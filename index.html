HCT LSC 1503 – LPA 4

Reflection: Interview Preparation and Partner Practice

Before you start

This week, you prepared for your interview assessment. You wrote your script for the first two questions, and then you worked with a partner and rehearsed your answers out loud.

Answer the questions below in complete sentences. Be honest about your experience — there are no right or wrong answers here. Aim for three to four sentences per question.

1 What did you learn this week about writing a script for an interview answer?

Think about: how you started your answer, how you organised your ideas, how you kept your answer to the right length, and how you made it sound natural and not read aloud.

0 words
I learned that an interview answer should… To organise my ideas, I… Writing for an interview is different from writing an essay because…
2 Which of your two answers was easier to write, and which was more difficult? Why?

Think about: finding ideas, choosing the right vocabulary, giving examples, and knowing what the interviewer wants to hear.

0 words
The easier answer to write was… because… The more difficult one was… because… I was not sure how to…, so I…
3 When you practised with your partner, what did your partner say about your answers? What advice will you use?

Think about: the feedback you received on your words, your voice, your eye contact and your body language.

0 words
My partner told me that… The best advice I received was… I will use this advice by…
4 What did you notice about your own speaking when you rehearsed your script out loud?

Think about: speed, pauses, confidence, pronunciation, and whether you depended too much on your paper.

0 words
When I said my answer out loud, I noticed that… Speaking was different from reading because… The words that were difficult to pronounce were…
5 What is one thing you will improve before the real interview, and how will you practise it?

Think about: one clear, specific change, and when and how you will work on it.

0 words
The one thing I want to improve is… I will practise this by… I will do this… times before the interview.
